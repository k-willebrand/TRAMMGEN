# TRAMMGEN

 A stochastic stochastic weather generation approach that integrates a k-nearest-neighbor (k-NN) resampling approach with a transient climate perturbation module derived from ensembles of global climate models (GCMs) to generate transient, multisite, multivariable climate scenarios. The k-NN component generates spatially coherent, multivariate weekly temperature and precipitation sequences by resampling historical observations, representing internal climate variability. The perturbation module applies time-evolving changes in the mean and variability of temperature and precipitation from CMIP6 projections across multiple shared socioeconomic pathways, enabling transient climate trajectories while preserving the dependence structure of the stochastic generator. 

## Features
* The k-NN component generates spatially coherent, multivariate weekly temperature and precipitation sequences by resampling historical observations, representing internal climate variability.
* The transient perturbation module applies time-evolving changes in the mean and variability of temperature and precipitation from CMIP6 projections across multiple shared socioeconomic pathways, enabling transient climate trajectories while preserving the dependence structure of the stochastic generator.
